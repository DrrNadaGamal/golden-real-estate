How to choose the right neighborhood for your family

Compare schools, services and transport before you compare prices.

← All articles
TipsNovember 12, 20264 min readاقرأ بالعربية

Start with your daily life

Before opening any listing, write down what your family really needs: nearby schools, a club, a hospital, or a fast road to work. A short list protects you from falling for a beautiful flat in the wrong place.

Visit at different times

Go in the morning at school run, in the evening after work, and on a weekend. You will see the traffic, the noise and the parking, none of which show up in photos.

Compare services before price

A cheaper price per square meter can mean a longer daily trip to school or the supermarket. Add the weekly travel time and cost to the final price.

Ask the neighbors

A long-time resident gives you what an agent will not: water cuts, safety at night, and how the building is managed.
