Rent or buy: which suits you?

Compare the monthly payment with rent, upkeep and your plans for the coming years.

← All articles
FinanceOctober 28, 20264 min readاقرأ بالعربية

Count the full cost

Buying is not just the installment: there is a down payment, registration fees, upkeep and insurance. Add them up and compare with the monthly rent for the same space.

Think about how long you will stay

If you will stay in the same city for more than five years, buying is usually better. If your plans may change, renting gives you flexibility.

Keep a cash reserve

Do not put all your savings into the down payment. Keep enough for six months of expenses so the installment never becomes a burden.
