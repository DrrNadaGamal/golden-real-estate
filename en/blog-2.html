Before you buy: a property inspection checklist

Check damp, electrics, plumbing and paperwork before you sign.

← All articles
BuyingNovember 5, 20265 min readاقرأ بالعربية

Check the visible condition

Look for damp stains on ceilings and low on the walls, try every door and window, and run the taps to check pressure and drainage.

Electrics and plumbing

Ask for the electrical panel to be inspected, confirm there is grounding, and note the electricity, water and gas meters. Any major repair belongs in the price.

Review the paperwork

Confirm the title, the registration and that the unit has no claims on it. Ask for maintenance records and any fees owed on the unit.

Do not rush the signature

Read the whole contract and have a lawyer review it before you pay a deposit. A good deal can wait a day or two.
